# Netflix Historical 10-K Filings

This folder contains Netflix's historical Form 10-K annual
reports used to support the FIN 439 Project 1 financial
analysis and valuation.

These filings provide historical financial statements,
accounting disclosures, and supporting information for
the five-year financial forecast and DCF valuation.

The primary valuation date is January 23, 2026.

**Note:** The FY2025 Form 10-K was filed after the
January 23 market close. Financial inputs used in the
historical valuation must be reconciled to information
available by the stated cutoff.
