# Netflix: WATCH–DEFER

**Recommendation: defer initiation at the $86.12 reference price.**

Base estimated value: **about $51/share**
Operating-case range: **about $42–$60/share**

Committee scenario: fund holds no position · Horizon: 2026–2030

Historical retrospective comparison with January 23, 2026 close.
Pending WBD transaction excluded; no current trade recommendation.

Source: `final/visible_results.md`; revised assumptions

---

# The business opportunity

Netflix's FY2025 results show why it deserves research:

- Revenue: **$45.18bn**, up **16%**
- Operating profit: **$13.33bn**, up **28%**
- Membership growth, pricing and advertising supported revenue

The investment case requires durable growth in cash after content spending.

Source: FY2025 10-K, Item 7; Edition A

---

# Spending determines the cash investors receive

FY2025 content investment: **$17.10bn**
Content expense through amortization: **$16.42bn**

The model removes a **$619m** charge from recurring costs,
but retains the expected **$700m** cash deposit in 2026.

**Cash obligations still matter when reported earnings improve.**

Source: FY2025 10-K, cash-flow statement/Note 9; model normalization

# The base case already assumes strong execution

| Base expectation | 2026 | 2030 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue growth | 12% | 6% |
| Content-investment growth | 8% | 4% |
| Revenue | $50.61bn | $68.19bn |
| Cash available to capital providers | $10.93bn | $18.00bn |

2030 operating margin reaches **35.4%**.
More expensive content or weaker pricing would challenge that result.

Source: `final/visible_results.md`; annual unlevered cash flow (FCFF)

# What would have to go better?

The reverse valuation requires approximately **$100bn** of 2030 revenue
to support $86.12, versus **$68bn** in the base case.

Content growth and other assumptions are held fixed in that experiment.

- About **75%** of base enterprise value depends on the long run
- Separate rate/growth sensitivity: **$41.73–$69.45/share**
- Structural impairment stress: **$15.57/share**

These experiments are conditional; they have no assigned probabilities.

Source: saved reverse DCF, sensitivity grid and stress case

---

# Cross-checks and unresolved transaction risk

Disney-based references: **about $41/share on P/E**
and **about $25/share on EV/revenue**.

Different businesses and tax effects limit their weight.
One qualified peer provides no reliable peer median.

**The pending WBD transaction needs separate analysis:**
financing, dilution, integration costs and combined cash generation.

Source: `peer_reference()`; final peer policy and standalone scope

---

# Committee action and review triggers

**Keep Netflix on watch; allocate no capital on this evidence.**

- Revisit entry below supported value with an explicit margin of safety
- Rebuild valuation if stronger cash generation has evidence behind it
- Review annual growth, margins and content payments after earnings
- Growth below the **10%** first-year low case triggers deeper review
- Persistent **0–2%** growth with sticky spending challenges watch status

Any initiation also requires transaction underwriting and reconciled data timing.

Source: model cases; proposed analyst monitoring rules, seasonality considered
